Use these as base prompts in your image/video tool of choice. Keep the **identity lock** block identical across generations for consistency.

```

signal-teal casual jacket over charcoal tee, soft stubborn cowlick in short dark hair,
warm curious expression, subtle freckles, clean editorial lighting (cool teal key + warm amber rim),
```
